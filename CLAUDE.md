# Lost Tribe Knowledge — documentary production

Documentaries for the YouTube channel @LostTribeKnowledge, one folder per film in `productions/`.

- Follow `studio/STANDARD_WORKFLOW.md` (the owner's approved standard procedure) and see `studio/TOOLS.md` for the
  toolset. In a new session run `sh studio/setup_tools.sh` first.
- Archival first; 2.5D moves (`ARD`) on archival photographs; HyperFrames (`MV`) for motion graphics; Blender
  reconstructions only where no authentic picture exists, labelled as such.
- Never present AI-generated imagery as historical evidence; never label synthetic narration as a human voice.
- No paid services. No YouTube upload or publication without the owner's explicit approval for that step.
- Do not commit master films to git without the owner's go-ahead (each adds ~1.6 GB).
- The owner writes in Serbian; reply in Serbian. Narration and on-screen text are in American English.
